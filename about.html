Who We Are

From a camera to a
full creative agency.

Xpo Imaging Limited is a fully registered Ugandan creative communications, marketing, branding, and media production company established in 2014. What began as a professional video production and photography company is now a full-service marketing and branding agency providing integrated communication solutions to government institutions, development partners, NGOs, UN agencies, corporate organizations, financial institutions, and private businesses.

We specialize in helping organizations communicate their impact, strengthen their brands, influence public perception, and engage stakeholders through strategic marketing, creative branding, digital communications, professional photography, documentary filmmaking, and visual storytelling.

Our experienced team of marketers, communication specialists, filmmakers, photographers, graphic designers, content creators, and digital strategists combines creativity with strategic thinking to develop compelling campaigns that produce measurable results.

Having worked extensively across Uganda, including underserved and hard-to-reach communities such as Nyumanzi Refugee Settlement, Karamoja, Moroto, Kaabong, Abim, Pader, Oyam, Kitagata, Amuru, Adjumani, and other regions, we understand how to tell authentic stories that inspire action, influence policy, mobilize resources, and strengthen institutional visibility.
